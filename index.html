01 · Developmental Editing
Developmental
Editing
The big-picture pass — structure, story, and whether the whole thing works.
What it is
Evaluating the entire manuscript's architecture — plot, pacing, character arcs.
When
After your first complete draft. Fix the house before the paint.
Passes
1–2 passes. Big changes cascade, don't overdo it.
01
Take a 1–2 week break after finishing
The gap creates the objectivity you need. This isn't optional.
02
Read in as few sittings as possible
Diagnose — don't fix. Take brief margin notes only.
03
Build a scene-by-scene outline of what you wrote
Not what you meant — what's on the page. Gaps appear instantly.
Dev Edit Checklist
Every chapter earns its place — cut what doesn't advance plot or character
The opening hooks and the ending pays off
Character motivations are clear and consistent throughout
Pacing: the midpoint doesn't sag, the ending doesn't rush

Copyediting
Consistency, accuracy, and grammar — after the structure is locked.
What it is
Correcting grammar, punctuation, and consistency in style and names.
When
After the dev edit. Don't copyedit chapters you might delete.
Passes
1–2 targeted passes. One for consistency, one tool-assisted.
01
Create your style sheet
Every character name, place, made-up word, and style rule.
02
Fact-check every real-world reference
One wrong date tells readers you're sloppy.
03
Run a global find for your most-common errors
You have patterns. Find them and fix every instance.
ProWritingAid
Style issues at scale. Better than Grammarly for books.
RECOMMENDED
Chicago Manual
The standard for fiction and most nonfiction.

03 · Line Editing
Line Editing
The craft pass. Every sentence earns its oxygen or gets cut.
What it is
Improving sentences — clarity, rhythm, voice, and flow.
When
After copyediting. Your final writing pass.
Passes
1 slow pass. Budget 1–2 weeks for a novel.
01
Read the whole manuscript aloud — slowly
Your ear catches what your eye skips. Use TTS if needed.
02
Cut every sentence that could be deleted without loss
If it does no work — no beat, no information — delete it.
03
Replace weak verbs and passive constructions
'Was running' → 'ran'. Find every 'was' and ask if there's better.
Common traps
The three line-editing sins: (1) over-explaining, (2) head-hopping in close-third POV, (3) starting scenes with weather or waking up.
Line Edit Checklist
Every paragraph has a clear purpose — information, emotion, or movement
Adverbs audited — replace or delete most of them
Sentence rhythm varies — no drone of same-length sentences

04 · Proofreading
Proofreading
The final catch. Errors only — no more writing.
What it is
Catching remaining typos, misspellings, and missing words.
When
Last — after all edits, on the formatted file you'll publish.
Passes
2 minimum — one by you, one by someone else.
01
Proofread the formatted file, not the Word doc
Formatting introduces new errors. Read what readers will actually see.
02
Use a ruler or card to read one line at a time
Forces you to process each line individually instead of skimming.
03
Check all front and back matter separately
Copyright page, ToC, author bio. The most embarrassing errors live here.
Proofreading Checklist
Title page: title, subtitle, author name — consistent with cover
Copyright page: year, name, ISBN/ASIN, edition — all accurate
Table of contents matches actual chapter titles and page numbers
No doubled words ('the the') or missing words anywhere

05 · Formatting ★ Bonus
Formatting
Making it look like a real book — print and ebook interior layout.
What it is
Designing the interior — typeface, margins, chapter headers, final files.
When
After all editing. Always last. Changes after formatting = formatting again.
DIY?
Ebook: very feasible. Print: use Vellum or Affinity Publisher.
Vellum
Beautiful output, near-zero learning curve. Mac only. ~$250.
BEST FOR MOST
Atticus
Cross-platform Windows + Mac. Writing + formatting. ~$147.
BEST FOR WINDOWS
Affinity
Pro-grade layout, total control, steeper curve. ~$70.
CONTROL FREAKS
KDP Built-in
Free, limited, mediocre. Fine for a first experiment only.
LAST RESORT
Honest budget take
If you're choosing between $150 on Vellum and $150 on a formatter, buy Vellum. The cost per book drops to near zero by book three.
Formatting Checklist — Print
Trim size confirmed and matched to KDP/IngramSpark specs
Body font is a readable serif at 10.5–12pt
Chapter openers start on a right-hand (recto) page
